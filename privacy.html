Privacy Policy — Net Payout Calculator

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · Publisher: c_tan2525

Information we collect

Net Payout Calculator does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The fee rates you edit, any custom marketplaces you add, and your language preference are saved only in your device's local storage so the app can remember your settings. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us or to any third party, and is removed when you clear the app's data or reset the settings to defaults inside the app.

Network use

The app loads its own program files when installed or updated. Calculations are performed entirely on your device. The app contains links to external websites (for example, spreadsheet products offered by the publisher); those websites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave the app.

Children

The app is a general-purpose calculator and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: ky0@muse.ai
